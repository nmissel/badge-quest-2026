✦ NIGHT LEGEND ✦

The night sky, and a small ceremony for every badge. Ride to a tablet and press AWAKEN.

Steps: every finished quest gives your hero 8 steps. Green arrows are steps you have, red ones you don't yet. Out of steps? Your hero camps, and wakes each new day with at least 5 steps. Steps never expire.

Partner & team: they aren't separate maps. A partner adds the Fellowship Vale to the east, over the bridge; a team adds the Company Hold to the west. The camera follows your hero, and you can drag to look around.

Gear: find the sword to cut paths through forests, and a banner to see further into the night. Both show on your hero.

Badges: each one has its own awakening. Try the demo AWAKEN button on different tablets. Rarer badges get bigger scenes. Secrets appear right where you are.

Finds: ride over things that glint. Scrolls are torn pieces of map: each one lights up a patch of the night somewhere else, and the view swings over to show you.
